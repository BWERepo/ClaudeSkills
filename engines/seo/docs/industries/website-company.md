# Industry guide: Website design / development company

## Typical customer situations & intents
- **High-commercial / local**: "<city> web design", "website designer near me", "small business
  website designer <region>".
- **Service**: website redesign, e-commerce/online store site, website for a specific trade
  ("HVAC website design"), landing pages, website maintenance/care plans.
- **Cost**: "how much does a small business website cost", "website redesign cost" — commercial
  in effect; strongest when real prices can be shown.
- **Comparison**: custom site vs. template/builder (Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy), agency vs.
  freelancer vs. DIY. Honest comparisons convert; DIY-seekers mostly don't.
- **Problem / solution**: "website not getting leads", "website looks outdated", "site not
  mobile friendly", "can't update my website", "website is slow".
- **Informational**: what a small business website should include, domains vs. hosting — only
  when linked to a service/CTA.

## What the customer needs to see
Real examples (clearly separating live client sites from demos), price or price model, process
and timeline, who does the work, what's included (hosting, domain, email, SEO basics), ownership
of the site and accounts, maintenance options, risk reducers (free mock-up/prototype, no deposit)
— all only if true.

## Typical structure
```
Website design (home or /web-design)
 ├─ <Primary city> web design (local)            ├─ Pricing / what a website costs
 ├─ Website redesign / makeover                   │   └─ Redesign cost (section, not page, unless intent differs)
 │   ├─ Signs you need a redesign (support)       ├─ Custom vs. template/builder (comparison)
 │   └─ Why a site doesn't generate leads         ├─ E-commerce / online store websites
 ├─ Industries we build for (pillar)              └─ Maintenance / care plans
 │   └─ <Industry> website design (only with genuine industry knowledge/proof)
 └─ Portfolio / case studies (proof, labeled)
```

## Must be verified
Prices and what "from" means, turnaround times, who owns what after payment, hosting/domain
handling, number of revisions, guarantees, any client names/logos (permission), results claims
(traffic/lead increases need real data), certifications/partnerships.

## Conversions
Free consultation / mock-up / prototype request, quote request, call, contact form.

## Traps
- City pages that only swap names (doorway). Industry pages that only swap industry names.
- Portfolios mixing demos with client work without labels.
- Promising rankings or "SEO included" beyond what is actually delivered.
- Over-emphasizing tools (AI, frameworks) instead of customer outcomes.

## Schema
ProfessionalService (or Organization), Service per service page, BreadcrumbList, Article for
guides/case studies, Offer only for real published prices.
