# Conversion optimization

SEO traffic is not the goal; qualified customers are. The profile's `positioning.primaryConversion`
defines success (call, estimate request, booking, consultation, prototype request, purchase,
store visit, reservation, contact form).

## Every commercial page

- One primary CTA, repeated at natural decision points (after the opening, after proof/process,
  at the end). Secondary CTA (phone, email) for people who prefer it.
- CTA wording fits the page's intent and stage — vary it; don't paste identical text everywhere.
  A cost-guide reader and a ready-to-hire reader need different invitations.
- Remove friction: tappable phone links, short forms, clear "what happens next", no surprise
  payment asks, honest response-time expectations (verified only).
- Proof near the CTA: real work, labeled demos, process, guarantees that actually exist.

## Measurement hooks

- The lead/booking record should capture the landing page and referrer (first-touch) so organic
  leads can be attributed. If the project can't, record that as a gap in `SEO-MEASUREMENT.md`.
- A conversion event in analytics (e.g. `generate_lead`) fired on successful submit, not on click.

## Review questions

Would a qualified visitor know within 10 seconds what the business does, where, for whom, and how
to start? Is the next step obvious and low-risk? Is anything on the page making the business look
less real (placeholder copy, unlabeled demos, contradictory claims)?
