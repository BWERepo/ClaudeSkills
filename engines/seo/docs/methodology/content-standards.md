# Content standards (quality, honesty, voice)

Write for the business owner's customer first. Search engines second.

## Must

- Answer the searcher's question early and specifically.
- Use the business's real process, real prices (only if verified and approved for publishing),
  real examples, real service area, real constraints ("we don't do X").
- Match the profile's brand voice; plain, specific, business-owner-friendly language.
- Short paragraphs, descriptive headings, lists where they genuinely help scanning.
- Every factual claim traces to a verified profile fact (the **claims ledger** — see
  `page-creation.md`). Opinions and general industry knowledge are fine when phrased as such.
- Label demos, samples, mock-ups and illustrations as such, next to them, every time.
- Disclose relationships that affect trust (e.g. a case study for a family member's business).

## Never

- Keyword stuffing, repeated exact-match phrases, lists of towns, hidden text, text written only
  for bots, doorway pages.
- Fabricated: customers, case studies, testimonials, reviews, ratings, awards, certifications,
  licenses, locations/offices, employees, statistics, partnerships, business history, service
  capabilities, prices, response times, guarantees.
- Borrowed statistics without a named, linkable source. If a number can't be sourced, drop it.
- Ranking promises ("#1 on Google", "rank in 24 hours", "guaranteed page one").
- Copying or closely paraphrasing competitor content.
- Regulated-outcome claims (medical results, legal outcomes, financial returns).

## Banned filler phrases (rewrite if they appear)

"In today's digital landscape", "unlock your potential", "take your business to the next level",
"revolutionize", "whether you're a small business or a large enterprise", "look no further",
"one-stop shop", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "game-changer", "elevate your brand",
"in this article we will", "it's important to note that", "delve".

## Length

As long as the intent needs, no longer. A decision-stage service page can be 600–1,200 useful
words; a thorough cost guide may be longer. Never pad to hit a word count.

## AI assistance

AI may draft; a human-quality bar applies. Every draft is fact-checked against the profile, read
for tone, and cut for filler before QA.
