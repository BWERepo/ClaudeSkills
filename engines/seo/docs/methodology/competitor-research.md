# Competitor & SERP research (`/BWESEOCompetitors`)

Purpose: understand what the search result page rewards for an approved target topic — not to
copy anyone. Needs a web-search capability; if none is available, say so and skip.

## Procedure (per target topic, a handful of queries)

1. Run the head query plus 1–2 variants (with and without the location) in standard web search.
2. Record, without copying text:
   - Who ranks: local businesses, national agencies, directories/listicles, marketplaces,
     government/news. Directories dominating = hard to win a service page; consider a GBP/
     directory listing instead.
   - Page types: service page, city page, guide, listicle, tool, comparison.
   - Questions they answer, depth, whether prices are shown, proof shown (portfolio, reviews),
     local specifics, CTA style.
   - Gaps: what nobody answers well that this business can answer truthfully.
3. Note competitors' claims only as context. **Never** reuse their wording, structure verbatim,
   statistics, or images. Rankings are not proof of quality.
4. Put findings in the page map row (`SERP notes`) and, if substantial, in
   `docs/seo/SEO-STRATEGY.md` → Competitive landscape. Research-found competitors go into the
   profile's `competitors[]` with `source: "research:<date>"`, status `unverified`.

## Limits

- No scraping of full articles, no paid tools without approval, no volumes/difficulty invented
  from impressions of the SERP.
- Web search results can be regional or stale; say so when it matters.
