# AI search / answer-engine readiness

Make the business easy for people and automated systems to understand. The same things that help
humans help answer engines: clarity, facts, structure, originality.

## Do

- Clear, question-shaped headings where the page genuinely answers questions; answer in the first
  sentence under the heading, then elaborate.
- State core business facts plainly and consistently on every relevant page: what the business
  does, for whom, where, how to start, price model (if published).
- Semantic HTML (one H1, logical headings, lists, tables for comparisons), descriptive link text.
- Structured data that matches the page (`structured-data.md`), one consistent entity (`@id`).
- An "About" section/page with real people and real history (verified), and consistent NAP.
- Original material: the business's real process, real examples, real numbers it can stand behind.
- Useful FAQs drawn from real customer questions.

## Don't

- Hidden text, bot-only pages, "LLM manipulation" pages, entity-stuffed paragraphs, fake
  authority signals (made-up awards, invented experts, fake citations).
- Contradictions between pages (different prices, timelines, contact details) — they erode trust
  for humans and machines alike.
