## 1. Brief in five lines
Premium tabletop rentals (china, flatware, glassware, accessories) for weddings, corporate and private events in {primary city} and {secondary city}. Audience: couples, planners, coordinators, venues, corporate organisers. Primary conversion: build a wishlist and request a quote (inquiry, not checkout). Must feel like a luxury event company, not a generic store. No existing brand system found.

## 2. What the research showed
- Strong premium home-goods sites let photography carry the page; text is short and set in a refined serif.
- Collections are introduced as moods or occasions before they become product grids.
- The best inquiry flows feel like a consultation: few fields, clear next step, reassurance about response time.
- Wishlists that stay visible (count in the header, slide-out panel) keep browsing and inquiring connected.

## 4. Three design directions

|---|---|---|---|
| Philosophy | Quiet luxury; the table is the hero | Confident, magazine-like structure | Intimate, collected, personal |

## 5. Recommendation
A. Timeless Luxury, with B's grid discipline for collections and product pages: it matches the premium wedding audience, lets photography sell, and the grid keeps a growing catalog easy to browse.
