# Three design directions

The three directions must differ in **layout**, **visual hierarchy** and **brand personality**, not just colour. A useful test: if you swapped their palettes, could you still tell them apart? If not, they are variations of one template; redo one.

## Ways to separate them (pick a different answer per direction on at least three axes)

| Axis | Examples |
|---|---|
| Composition | Full-bleed editorial imagery · strict modular grid · layered, overlapping collage · split-screen · text-led |
| Hierarchy | Image first · headline first · product/catalog first · tool/action first |
| Type | High-contrast serif display · neo-grotesk sans · humanist sans · mixed serif+sans |
| Colour | Warm neutrals · monochrome plus one accent · soft pastels · deep, saturated |
| Density | Generous and calm · balanced · compact and information-rich |
| Navigation | Minimal top bar · mega menu · sticky category rail · bottom bar on mobile |
| Motion | Almost none · subtle reveal and hover · expressive but respectful of reduced-motion |

## For each direction

- Name and one-sentence philosophy
- Audience fit (who it wins, who it may lose)
- Palette: 5-7 HEX values with roles (background, surface, text, muted, accent, accent-contrast), and the main text/background contrast ratio
- Typography: display and body families (prefer fonts the project already has or freely licensed ones; say which), scale sketch
- Layout principles and homepage outline
- Navigation (desktop and mobile)
- Photography style (what to shoot or source; never stock that misrepresents the business)
- Component treatment (buttons, cards, forms, inputs)
- Interaction and animation (restrained by default; honour `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Mobile strategy
- Supporting references (shortlist ranks, with source labels)
- Advantages and trade-offs

Then **one recommendation** with reasons tied to the brief. A recommended combination ("A, with B's grid discipline") is fine if each part is explicit.

## Avoid

- Generic AI-looking layouts: centred hero + three icon cards + testimonial carousel by default, purple gradients, glassmorphism without reason.
- Oversized buttons, empty space without purpose, decoration that hides content.
- Inventing brand facts, testimonials, ratings or statistics for mock-ups; use clearly marked placeholders.
